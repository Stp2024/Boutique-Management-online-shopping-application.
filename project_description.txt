================================================================================
VASTRAÉ | DIGITAL COUTURE BOUTIQUE
Project Description & Architecture Overview
================================================================================

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VASTRAÉ | Digital Couture Boutique is a high-luxury Boutique Management & Online 
Shopping Web Application. It bridges traditional bespoke Indian & ethnic 
tailoring with modern e-commerce, AI virtual fitting rooms, and multi-role 
operations management (Customer, Master Tailor, Admin).


2. KEY MODULES & FEATURES BUILT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[A] E-Commerce Storefront & Shopping Experience
- Curated Storefront & Catalogue (shop, catalogue): Filterable product 
  collections (Couture, Bridal, Fusion, Gen-Z, Ready-to-Wear) with live search, 
  sorting, price range filters, and quick view.
- Sacred Traditional Pavilion (traditional): Dedicated heritage pavilion for 
  premium Indian ethnic wear (Sarees, Lehengas, Sherwanis, Anarkalis) featuring 
  interactive drape guides and Temple Sanctum aesthetics.
- Style Kits & Lookbooks (style-kits, lookbook): Curated signature style 
  bundles, festive event packages, and interactive visual trend lookbooks.
- Shopping Cart & Checkout (modals): Slide-out cart drawer, promo code 
  application, shipping calculator, and checkout workflow.

[B] Bespoke Customization & Tailoring Studio
- Custom Bespoke Studio (custom): Interactive visual custom outfit creator 
  allowing clients to select necklines, sleeve lengths, embroidery patterns, 
  fabric types, and embellishments.
- "Stitch Your Own Fabric" Service (own-fabric): Allows clients to upload photos/
  details of their own fabric, request doorstep fabric pickup, specify stitching 
  instructions, and get automated price estimates.
- Digital Measurement Manager (measurements, profile): Save and store precise 
  client body measurements, view step-by-step measurement guides, and save 
  multiple profile presets (e.g., Self, Family).
- Design Moodboard Builder (moodboard): Interactive canvas where clients can save 
  color swatches, inspiration images, and design ideas for custom orders.

[C] AI Virtual Fitting & Interactive Experience
- AI Virtual Mirror & Digital Twin (ai-mirror): Interactive digital mirror 
  simulation to preview outfit fits and color match recommendations.
- AI Self Try-On Lab (tryon): Virtual try-on module supporting photo uploads or 
  camera capture for virtual garment overlay.
- Virtual Atelier Consultations (experience): Consultation booking engine to 
  schedule video sessions with personal stylists and couture experts.

[D] Order Logistics & Real-Time Tracking
- Live Order & Stitching Tracker (tracking): Visual stage-by-stage progress 
  tracker showing exact order status:
  Fabric Sourcing -> Pattern Cutting -> Hand Embroidery -> Master Stitching -> 
  Quality Inspection -> Dispatched.

[E] Multi-Role Workspaces & Operations Management
- Multi-Role Authentication (auth, prelaunch): User registration and role switcher 
  (Customer, Master Tailor, Admin).
- Master Tailor Dashboard (tailor-workspace): Dedicated workspace for tailors 
  to view assigned custom orders, access client measurement sheets, update 
  stitching stages, and upload work-in-progress photos.
- Admin Management Studio (admin-workspace): Comprehensive management portal 
  to control product inventory, manage order lifecycles, assign orders to master 
  tailors, view sales analytics, and manage client accounts.

[F] Customer Care & Engagement
- Atelier Services (services): Express 24h Stitching, Doorstep Home Fitting, 
  Garment Alterations.
- Private Offers (offers): Exclusive loyalty discounts, festive flash sales, and 
  VIP client coupon redemption.
- Client Reviews & Testimonials (reviews): Verified client ratings, photo reviews, 
  and feedback displays.
- Interactive Help Desk (faq): Categorized accordion FAQ for orders, sizing, 
  delivery, and returns.


3. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Modular Component System: Single Page Application (SPA) architecture loading 
  29 modular HTML components on-demand (js/section-loader.js & js/sections.js).
- Design System & Aesthetics (css/styles.css): Premium luxury dark & gold theme, 
  glassmorphic cards, smooth micro-interactions, responsive grid layouts, and 
  custom typography (Playfair Display & DM Sans).
- State Management (js/app.js, js/products.js, js/features.js): Dynamic cart & 
  wishlist management, measurement profile persistence, custom order 
  configuration engine, and local storage sync.
