ABOUT VASTRAÉ ATELIER

OUR ATELIER PHILOSOPHY

Crafting clothes that remember you.

“A garment should remember the body it was made for. We don't believe in standard mass-production sizes.”

Located on 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru, VASTRAÉ is a single dedicated boutique. We are not an open marketplace or multi-vendor aggregator. Every garment ordered is drafted, cut, and stitched under one roof by generational Karnataka artisans.

From custom bridal maggam blouses and hand-tailored linen suits to our acclaimed Saree Reborn waste-to-best upcycling initiative, we blend traditional loom craftsmanship with modern digital ease.

⚠️
Mandatory Master Tailor Physical Inspection Policy:

Our shipping delivery team cannot judge fabric tensile strength. Every vintage saree must be inspected physically on our Indiranagar drafting loom. If the fabric is determined to be Not Usable due to brittle silk age, we inform you immediately and safely return the saree (customer pays applicable return shipping charges of ₹180).

COMMON INQUIRIES

Concierge VIP FAQ

Quick answers regarding salon visits, fitting turnaround times, fabric drop-offs, and bespoke consultations.

Do I need an appointment to visit the Indiranagar salon?

Walk-ins are warmly welcomed from Monday to Saturday (10:00 AM – 8:30 PM) for browsing fabrics and collections. For bridal trousseau consultations or calico fittings, reserving a time slot is recommended.

What is the typical turnaround time for custom tailoring?

Standard custom blouse and kurti stitching takes 48 to 72 hours. Hand-embroidered maggam bridal blouses require 5 to 7 days, and full wedding lehengas require 10 to 14 days. Express 24-hr rush service is available.

Can I bring my own fabric or heirloom saree?

Absolutely! You can drop off your fabric at our flagship salon or use our doorstep collection service across Bengaluru. Our master tailors physically inspect the weave and draft your custom pattern.

What if my garment needs sizing adjustments later?

Every garment crafted at VASTRAÉ includes generous 2-inch internal seam allowances and complimentary lifetime alteration privileges. Simply bring it in or request a courier pickup.
