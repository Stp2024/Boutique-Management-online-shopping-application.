Which Areas May Still Be Reusable?

🪡 Main Body Silk (~4.2 – 4.8m)

Ample strong silk available. Prime candidate for a full lehenga skirt, ethnic kurti, or tailored anarkali panels.

👑 Ornate Pallu Section (~0.9m)

Densely woven zari motifs. Recommended as a statement blouse back, jacket front yoke, or luxury heirloom cushion cover.

🎗️ Metallic Border Length (~8.5m)

Intact border strips will be carefully unstitched, hand-pressed, and repurposed along new hems, necklines, and cuffs.

✂️ Damaged / Worn Zones (~0.5 – 0.8m)

Frayed pleat lines will be strategically isolated and excluded from pattern cutting. No damaged silk will touch the final garment.

⚠️
Tailor Physical Loom Inspection Mandatory Policy Image-based results are preliminary visual estimates only. 🎗️ Border Utilization Strategy

Borders are unpicked by hand without slicing the warp. Repurposed as ornate hem borders on lehenga kalis, sleeve cuffs, or waistbands.

👑 Pallu Spotlight Placement

The dense zari pallu is placed on the most visible focal point: the back bodice of your blouse, the front yoke of an anarkali, or a statement jacket lapel.

🛡️ Preserving Valuable Embroidery & Zari

Antique silver/gold zari is secured with micro-interfacing and lined with pure cotton mulmul so metal threads never rub uncomfortably against skin.

⚠️ Damage Mitigation Strategy

When vintage fold lines or watermarks limit seamless cutting, tailor drafts flared kalis (panels) instead of a single piece, tucking flaws away.

AI Try-On Service Gateway

🔒
Strict Frontend Security Guarantee:

Secret AI API keys are stored solely on your secure local backend (backend_config.json or server environment variables) and never exposed in frontend JavaScript.

Active Engine Architecture: Atelier Photometric Engine (Biometric Synthesis)

The studio dynamically detects whether our Python backend (backend_server.py) has an active Neural AI key (e.g. IDM-VTON on Replicate or FASHN.ai). When no cloud key is provided, the high-fidelity VASTRAÉ Atelier Photometric Synthesis Engine executes client-side biometric calibration without placeholder deception.

How to Connect Real Neural AI Diffusion Services:

1
Launch the Secure Backend Gateway:

Run python backend_server.py in the project directory. The gateway runs on port 8080.

2
Supported Neural VTON Providers:

IDM-VTON (Replicate): Diffusion-based virtual try-on preserving garment textures.
FASHN.ai: Commercial high-resolution fashion garment warp API.
Google Gemini Vision / Multimodal: Multimodal prompt-guided clothing synthesis.

3
Configure Secret API Key in backend_config.json: 
{
  "provider": "replicate_idm_vton",
